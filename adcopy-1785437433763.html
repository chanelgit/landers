### 3 Full Ad Variations (Hook + PAS Body + CTA)

**Ad 1 — Pain Angle (matches Advertorial Bridge: "bad date/scam discovered too late")**

**Hook:** You matched. You talked for weeks. But do you actually know who they are?

*Problem:* Dating apps make it easy to fall for someone fast — but profiles can hide a lot: past relationships, records, even a different name.

*Agitate:* Sarah found that out after three dates. By the time she looked closer, she wished she'd checked sooner.

*Solution:* A quick search can show you what someone's profile won't.

**CTA:** See what a simple search reveals — before your next date.

---

**Ad 2 — Curiosity Angle (matches "I searched my own name" self-search demo)**

**Hook:** I searched my own name and couldn't believe what came up.

*Problem:* Most of us have no idea how much of our information is actually public — old addresses, records, accounts we forgot existed.

*Agitate:* If it's that easy to find your own history, imagine what's out there about the people around you.

*Solution:* One search shows you exactly what's on record — for you or anyone else.

**CTA:** Try searching a name and see for yourself.

---

**Ad 3 — Direct Benefit Angle (matches clean UI demo / speed)**

**Hook:** Full background report in 60 seconds. Criminal records, social profiles, and more.

*Problem:* Courthouse records, background check services, endless Googling — verifying someone the "official" way takes days and money.

*Agitate:* Meanwhile you're left guessing, hoping the person in front of you is who they say they are.

*Solution:* This tool pulls public records into one simple report, in about a minute.

**CTA:** Run your first search now — takes less time than reading this ad.
